# Hyundai Ioniq 5

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The Hyundai Ioniq 5 is a premium all-electric crossover SUV sold in India. It is built on Hyundai's dedicated Electric Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) architecture featuring an 800V ultra-fast charging system.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: Hyundai
- **Model**: Ioniq 5
- **Segment**: Premium Electric Crossover SUV
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹46.05 Lakh
- **Battery Capacity**: 72.6 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
- **Claimed Range (ARAI)**: 631 km on a single full charge
- **Real-world Estimated Range**: 450 km to 500 km depending on driving mode, terrain, and climate control
- **Drivetrain**: Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) with single permanent magnet synchronous motor
- **Motor Output**: 217 PS (160 kW) maximum power and 350 Nm peak torque
- **Top Speed**: 185 km/h
- **0-100 km/h Acceleration**: 7.6 seconds

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging (Home & Workplace)**: Supports standard Type 2 connector. An 11 kW AC 3-phase home wallbox charges from 10% to 100% in approximately 6 hours and 55 minutes. On a 7.2 kW single-phase wallbox, full charge takes approximately 10 to 11 hours.
- **DC Fast Charging**: Supports CCS2 connector with advanced 800V multi-charging architecture. On a 350 kW DC ultra-fast charger, it charges from 10% to 80% in just 18 minutes. On common 50 kW DC highway chargers, 10% to 80% takes approximately 57 minutes.
- **Vehicle-to-Load (V2L)**: Features bidirectional charging (V2L) inside and outside the vehicle delivering up to 3.6 kW power output (230V AC socket) to power laptops, camping gear, coffee machines, or emergency power tools.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Parametric Pixel LED headlamps and tail lamps.
- Sliding center console (Universal Island) and flat floor cabin space.
- Eco-friendly interior materials including eco-processed leather and bio-paint.
- Level 2 ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance System) with 21 safety features.
- Front trunk (frunk) with 57 litres storage capacity alongside 527 litres boot space.
