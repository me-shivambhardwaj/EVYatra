# BMW i7

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The BMW i7 is an electric vehicle available in the Indian market, manufactured by BMW. It offers an official claimed driving range of 625 km and represents modern electric mobility in India.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: BMW
- **Model**: i7
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹213.00 Lakh (₹2.13 Cr)
- **Battery Capacity**: 101.7 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
- **Claimed Range**: 625 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**: 437 km to 531 km depending on road conditions, payload, and driving style

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Standard Type 2 connector compatible. On a 7.2 kW AC home/workplace wallbox, a 10% to 100% full charge takes approximately 14.1 hours.
- **DC Fast Charging**: Supports CCS2 connector with up to 195 kW DC fast charging. Recharges from 10% to 80% in approximately 34 minutes on a compatible DC rapid highway charger.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Equipped with smart regenerative braking to maximize energy recovery in city traffic.
- Connected infotainment system with real-time battery analytics and range estimation.
- Engineered with IP67-rated waterproof battery pack and safety systems suited to Indian road environments.
