# EV Charger Types in India

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview of Charging Standards in India
In India, the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and Ministry of Power have established standardized charging protocols for public and private electric passenger four-wheelers (4W).

## Connector Types for 4W Electric Vehicles

### 1. CCS2 (Combined Charging System Type 2)
- **Usage**: The official universal standard for DC fast charging in India for all passenger electric cars (including Tata, Hyundai, MG, Mahindra, Kia, BYD, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, and Audi).
- **Connectors**: Combines standard Type 2 AC pin layout with two dedicated high-current DC pins directly below.
- **Power Rating**: Widely deployed from 25 kW, 30 kW, 50 kW, 60 kW, 120 kW, 150 kW, up to 350 kW DC ultra-rapid chargers across national highway corridors (such as Tata Power, Statiq, Jio-bp pulse, Zeon, and ChargeZone).
- **Speed**: Charges typical 4W battery packs from 10% to 80% in approximately 18 to 60 minutes depending on the vehicle's intake capacity and battery thermal management.

### 2. Type 2 (Mennekes) AC Connector
- **Usage**: The universal standard for AC charging (single-phase 230V and three-phase 415V AC) across home wallboxes, destination chargers, shopping malls, hotels, and office parking spaces in India.
- **Power Rating**: Typically 3.3 kW (slow portable), 7.2 kW (single-phase home wallbox), 11 kW, or 22 kW AC (three-phase).
- **Speed**: AC charging replenishes batteries steadily; a full 10% to 100% charge takes approximately 4 to 12 hours depending on onboard charger capacity.

### 3. Domestic 15A 3-Pin Portable Socket (Level 1)
- **Usage**: Standard 230V 15-amp Indian domestic power socket using portable charging cables provided by car manufacturers.
- **Power Rating**: Approximately 2.3 kW to 3.3 kW AC.
- **Speed**: Delivers 10 to 15 km of range per hour of charging. Ideal for overnight home charging and emergency top-ups.

### 4. Bharat DC-001 & Bharat AC-001 (Legacy Indian Fleet Standard)
- **Usage**: Low-voltage standard designed primarily for electric 2-wheelers, 3-wheelers, and early generation fleet electric cars.
- **Power Rating**: 15 kW DC (using GB/T connector format at 48V-72V low voltage).
- **Compatibility**: Not compatible with modern high-voltage passenger electric vehicles (which use CCS2).

### 5. CHAdeMO
- **Usage**: Japanese DC fast-charging standard. Rarely deployed in India; modern Indian passenger EVs do not utilize this connector.
