# BYD Atto 3

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The BYD Atto 3 is an electric vehicle available in the Indian market, manufactured by BYD. It offers an official claimed driving range of 521 km and represents modern electric mobility in India.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: BYD
- **Model**: Atto 3
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹24.99 Lakh
- **Battery Capacity**: 60.5 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
- **Claimed Range**: 521 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**: 364 km to 442 km depending on road conditions, payload, and driving style

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Standard Type 2 connector compatible. On a 7.2 kW AC home/workplace wallbox, a 10% to 100% full charge takes approximately 8.4 hours.
- **DC Fast Charging**: Supports CCS2 connector with up to 88 kW DC fast charging. Recharges from 10% to 80% in approximately 50 minutes on a compatible DC rapid highway charger.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Equipped with smart regenerative braking to maximize energy recovery in city traffic.
- Connected infotainment system with real-time battery analytics and range estimation.
- Engineered with IP67-rated waterproof battery pack and safety systems suited to Indian road environments.
