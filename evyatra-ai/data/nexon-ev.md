# Tata Nexon EV

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The Tata Nexon EV is India's leading compact electric SUV, manufactured by Tata Passenger Electric Mobility (TPEM). It is engineered on Tata's acti.ev / high-voltage Ziptron electric powertrain architecture.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: Tata Motors
- **Model**: Nexon EV (Medium Range and Long Range variants)
- **Segment**: Compact Electric SUV
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹12.49 Lakh to ₹17.19 Lakh
- **Battery Capacity**:
  - Medium Range (MR): 30 kWh liquid-cooled LFP battery pack
  - Long Range (LR): 45 kWh liquid-cooled LFP battery pack
- **Claimed Range (ARAI / MIDC)**:
  - Medium Range: 325 km
  - Long Range: 489 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**:
  - Medium Range: 210 km to 240 km
  - Long Range: 310 km to 360 km
- **Motor Output**:
  - Medium Range: 129 PS (95 kW) and 215 Nm torque
  - Long Range: 144 PS (106 kW) and 215 Nm torque
- **0-100 km/h Acceleration**: Approximately 8.9 seconds (Long Range)

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Uses universal Type 2 connector. Standard 3.3 kW portable home socket charger takes 10% to 100% in 10.5 hours (MR) / 15 hours (LR). Fast 7.2 kW AC home wallbox takes 10% to 100% in 4.3 hours (MR) / 6 hours (LR).
- **DC Fast Charging**: Uses CCS2 connector. On a 50 kW DC public fast charger, charges from 10% to 80% in approximately 56 minutes.
- **V2V and V2L Capability**: Long Range variants support Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) charging up to 3.3 kW and Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) external power output up to 3.3 kW.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Multi-mode regenerative braking with paddle shifters (4 levels).
- 190 mm ground clearance engineered for Indian road conditions.
- IP67-rated water and dust resistant battery pack and electric motor.
- Connected vehicle features via Tata ZConnect suite with smartwatch integration.
- Boot space of 350 litres.
