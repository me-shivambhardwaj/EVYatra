# MG Comet EV

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The MG Comet EV is an electric vehicle available in the Indian market, manufactured by MG. It offers an official claimed driving range of 230 km and represents modern electric mobility in India.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: MG
- **Model**: Comet EV
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹7.00 Lakh
- **Battery Capacity**: 17.3 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
- **Claimed Range**: 230 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**: 161 km to 195 km depending on road conditions, payload, and driving style

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Standard Type 2 connector compatible. On a 7.2 kW AC home/workplace wallbox, a 10% to 100% full charge takes approximately 5.2 hours.
- **DC Fast Charging**: Primarily designed for standard AC charging via Type 2 connector (approx 4 to 6 hours for full recharge). Does not support high-voltage DC fast charging.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Equipped with smart regenerative braking to maximize energy recovery in city traffic.
- Connected infotainment system with real-time battery analytics and range estimation.
- Engineered with IP67-rated waterproof battery pack and safety systems suited to Indian road environments.
