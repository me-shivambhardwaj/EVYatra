# EV Charging Costs and Range Tips in India

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Typical Charging Costs in India

### Home AC Charging (Most Economical)
- **Domestic Electricity Tariff**: Generally ranges from ₹6 to ₹9 per unit (kWh) across Indian state DISCOMs (e.g., Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat).
- **Cost per Full Charge**:
  - 30 kWh battery (e.g. Nexon EV MR): Approx ₹180 to ₹270.
  - 45 kWh battery (e.g. Nexon EV LR): Approx ₹270 to ₹405.
  - 72.6 kWh battery (e.g. Ioniq 5): Approx ₹435 to ₹650.
- **Running Cost per Kilometre**: Approximately ₹0.80 to ₹1.20 per km (compared to ₹7 to ₹9 per km for petrol cars).

### Public DC Fast Charging (Highway Corridors)
- **Commercial Rates**: Typically ₹16 to ₹24 per unit (kWh) + 18% GST on networks like Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, Jio-bp pulse, Zeon Electric, and ChargeZone.
- **Cost per 10-80% Session**:
  - Nexon EV / Punch EV (approx 20-30 kWh): Approx ₹380 to ₹720.
  - Ioniq 5 (approx 50 kWh): Approx ₹950 to ₹1,400.
- **Running Cost per Kilometre on Highway DC**: Approximately ₹2.00 to ₹3.00 per km.

## Best Practices & Cost Optimization Tips
1. **Prefer Home Charging**: Do 85-90% of your charging at home or office at residential tariffs to maximize cost savings.
2. **Utilize Time-of-Day (ToD) Tariffs**: Take advantage of reduced off-peak or solar electricity rates if your state electricity board offers EV concessions.
3. **80% Rule on DC Fast Chargers**: Disconnect once the battery reaches 80% SoC. Charging speed drops sharply after 80% to protect battery health, making remaining charging time and cost less efficient.
4. **Pre-condition While Plugged In**: Cool or warm your EV cabin using wallbox power before starting your trip to conserve battery range.
5. **Smooth Driving & Regenerative Braking**: Use moderate acceleration and set regenerative braking to medium in stop-and-go traffic to recover up to 15-20% of energy.
