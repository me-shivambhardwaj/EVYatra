# Audi Q8 e-tron

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The Audi Q8 e-tron is an electric vehicle available in the Indian market, manufactured by Audi. It offers an official claimed driving range of 600 km and represents modern electric mobility in India.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: Audi
- **Model**: Q8 e-tron
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹115.00 Lakh (₹1.15 Cr)
- **Battery Capacity**: 114.0 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
- **Claimed Range**: 600 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**: 420 km to 510 km depending on road conditions, payload, and driving style

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Standard Type 2 connector compatible. On a 7.2 kW AC home/workplace wallbox, a 10% to 100% full charge takes approximately 15.8 hours.
- **DC Fast Charging**: Supports CCS2 connector with up to 170 kW DC fast charging. Recharges from 10% to 80% in approximately 31 minutes on a compatible DC rapid highway charger.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Equipped with smart regenerative braking to maximize energy recovery in city traffic.
- Connected infotainment system with real-time battery analytics and range estimation.
- Engineered with IP67-rated waterproof battery pack and safety systems suited to Indian road environments.
