# Tata Curvv EV

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The Tata Curvv EV is an electric vehicle available in the Indian market, manufactured by Tata. It offers an official claimed driving range of 502 km and represents modern electric mobility in India.

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: Tata
- **Model**: Curvv EV
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹17.50 Lakh
- **Battery Capacity**: 55.0 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
- **Claimed Range**: 502 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**: 351 km to 426 km depending on road conditions, payload, and driving style

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Standard Type 2 connector compatible. On a 7.2 kW AC home/workplace wallbox, a 10% to 100% full charge takes approximately 7.6 hours.
- **DC Fast Charging**: Supports CCS2 connector with up to 70 kW DC fast charging. Recharges from 10% to 80% in approximately 40 minutes on a compatible DC rapid highway charger.

## Key Features & Practicality
- Equipped with smart regenerative braking to maximize energy recovery in city traffic.
- Connected infotainment system with real-time battery analytics and range estimation.
- Engineered with IP67-rated waterproof battery pack and safety systems suited to Indian road environments.
