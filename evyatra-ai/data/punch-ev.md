# Tata Punch EV

> Demo data: verify before commercial reliance.

## Overview
The Tata Punch EV is an entry-level compact electric SUV in India, built on Tata's dedicated pure EV platform named acti.ev (Advanced Connected Tech-Intelligent Electric Vehicle architecture).

## Specifications (India Spec)
- **Brand**: Tata Motors
- **Model**: Punch EV (Standard and Long Range options)
- **Segment**: Micro/Sub-compact Electric SUV
- **Ex-showroom Price (India)**: Approximately ₹9.99 Lakh to ₹14.29 Lakh
- **Battery Capacity**:
  - Standard (MR): 25 kWh liquid-cooled battery pack
  - Long Range (LR): 35 kWh liquid-cooled battery pack
- **Claimed Range (MIDC)**:
  - Standard: 315 km
  - Long Range: 421 km
- **Real-world Estimated Range**:
  - Standard: 200 km to 220 km
  - Long Range: 270 km to 300 km
- **Motor Output**:
  - Standard: 82 PS (60 kW) power and 114 Nm peak torque
  - Long Range: 122 PS (90 kW) power and 190 Nm peak torque
- **0-100 km/h Acceleration**: Approximately 9.5 seconds (Long Range)

## Charging Capabilities
- **Connectors**: CCS2, Type 2
- **AC Charging**: Type 2 connector compatible. Standard 3.3 kW AC home charger replenishes 10% to 100% in approx 9.4 hours (Standard) / 13.5 hours (LR). Optional 7.2 kW AC fast wallbox charges 10% to 100% in 3.6 hours (Standard) / 5 hours (LR).
- **DC Fast Charging**: Front-mounted CCS2 charging port behind the Tata badge. Supports 50 kW DC fast chargers, taking 10% to 80% in approximately 56 minutes.
- **Frunk Storage**: Features a convenient 14-litre front trunk (frunk) for storing the charging cable.

## Key Features & Practicality
- 190 mm unladen ground clearance with 350 mm water wading capability.
- 360-degree surround view camera and blind spot monitor.
- Electronic parking brake with auto-hold function.
- Multi-drive modes (City, Eco, Sport) with 4-level paddle-shift regenerative braking.
